# 烟火凡人心：工作方块 / Flavor Immersed Daily: Work Blocks

本模组是《烟火凡人心》的官方工作方块附属模组，把全部烹饪与加工设备集中到这里：
蒸笼、炒锅、高压锅、砧板、炸锅、变温烤箱、压榨机、坛子、茶壶、汤锅、研钵、绞肉机。

This is the official work-block add-on for **Flavor Immersed Daily**. It gathers all cooking and
processing stations in one place: steamer, wok, pressure cooker, chopping board, deep fryer,
temperature-controlled oven, press, pickling jar, teapot, soup pot, mortar and meat grinder.

## 中文

### 内容

- 12 种工作方块：蒸笼、炒锅、高压锅、砧板、炸锅、变温烤箱、压榨机、坛子、茶壶、汤锅、研钵、绞肉机。
- 砧板支持切割、包裹/成型、擀面与小型生物屠宰。
- 变温烤箱支持「烘干」与「烤箱」两种模式。
- 菜肴带火候、调味、厨师与自定义名称/描述。
- 首次进入世界会发放「教程卷轴」。
- 内置 JEI 与 EMI 配方查看支持。

### 依赖

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x
- 主模组《烟火凡人心》（Flavor Immersed Daily）
- 可选：Patchouli（帕秋莉手册），用于阅读教程卷轴

### 安装

把本模组与主模组一起放进 `mods` 文件夹即可。

## English

### Contents

- 12 work blocks: steamer, wok, pressure cooker, chopping board, deep fryer, temperature-controlled
  oven, press, pickling jar, teapot, soup pot, mortar and meat grinder.
- The chopping board handles cutting, wrapping/shaping, rolling and small-mob butchery.
- The temperature-controlled oven offers drying and oven modes.
- Dishes track cooking level, seasoning, chef, plus custom name and description.
- A tutorial scroll is granted on first join.
- JEI and EMI recipe viewing are supported.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x
- Flavor Immersed Daily (main mod)
- Optional: Patchouli, required to read the tutorial scroll

### Installation

Drop this mod and the main mod into your `mods` folder.
